# Ghosts in the Genealogy

A worked study on the rvDNA engine: recovering two extinct hominin lineages from
present-day genomes alone, with no ancient DNA and no reference genome for
either.

**[→ Open the illustrated report](index.html)** (self-contained HTML, animated
diagrams, light and dark).

---

## What this responds to

On **30 July 2026**, Zhang, Biddanda, Johnson, O'Dushlaine and Moorjani
published *Recovering signatures of archaic hominin introgression using
ancestral recombination graphs* in **Science**. Their method — **TRACE**
(TRacking Archaic Contributions via ARG Estimation) — reconstructs ancestral
recombination graphs across present-day genomes and looks for regions whose
ancestry reaches unusually far back in time. It recovered two previously unknown
"ghost" lineages:

| Lineage | Diverged | Route into us | Who carries it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deep African ghost | ~800 ka | Direct, inside Africa, >50 ka | Everyone, 0.5–1% of the genome |
| Super-archaic ghost | ~1.8 Ma | Via Denisovans | Oceania-biased, second-hand |

Neither has ever been sequenced. Both are visible only in the shape of living
people's genealogies.

## What is in here

This directory holds a first-principles reimplementation of that *logic* on
rvDNA — not of TRACE itself — validated against a simulated cohort where the
truth is known.

## Reproducing it

Everything is seeded. The same checkout produces the same numbers.

```bash
cargo build --release
./target/release/depth-hist artifacts/data   # ~5 s
./target/release/trace-rv   artifacts/data   # ~10 min, Darwin dominates
node artifacts/build.mjs
```

## The method in four steps

1. **Simulate a cohort with known truth.** A structured (multispecies)
   coalescent runs per window on a hominin population tree calibrated to the
   published divergence times. Introgression is planted by reassigning a lineage
   to the donor deme — which is exactly what admixture means backwards in time.
2. **Ask one question of every segment.** How far back does this haplotype have
   to go before it meets anybody else? Segments donated by a lineage that split
   800 ka cannot meet anyone until they get back past that split, because until
   then there was nobody else to meet.
3. **Make that question cheap.** Each window is embedded as an rvDNA k-mer
   profile vector and indexed in a RuVector HNSW graph. The index decides *what
   to look at*; exact Jukes–Cantor-corrected divergence — which decides the
   answer — runs only against the candidates it returns.
4. **Tune and refine mechanically.** Darwin mode mutates the detector's own
   hyperparameters behind a margin gate and reports on a held-out split. The
   flywheel promotes confirmed ghost segments to references and re-runs until it
   goes dry.

## Results

### The cohort

58 modern haplotypes across nine populations plus 4 sequenced archaic references
(3 Neanderthal, 1 Denisovan); 360 independent windows of 2,000 bp each. The
mutation rate is rescaled ~109×, so one simulated window carries the
segregating-site information of about **217 kb** of real human sequence. Planted
archaic burden: **2.61%** of modern segments — 238 Neanderthal, 165 Ghost-A, 125
Denisovan, 18 Ghost-B.

### The clock holds

Estimated versus true TMRCA over 176 sampled pairs: **r = 0.9964**, mean relative
error **7.3%**, median true 684 ka against 690 ka estimated.

### Depth separates the classes

Coalescent depth to the nearest relative in the modern panel, measured
exhaustively:

| Segment truth | n | 5th pct | Median | 95th pct |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| No archaic ancestry | 20,334 | 0 ka | **32 ka** | 183 ka |
| Denisovan | 125 | 382 ka | 615 ka | 1,187 ka |
| Neanderthal | 238 | 421 ka | 690 ka | 1,678 ka |
| Ghost-A (deep African) | 165 | 713 ka | 980 ka | 2,466 ka |
| Ghost-B (super-archaic) | 18 | 1,932 ka | **2,480 ka** | 4,598 ka |

The best a single depth threshold can do is **410 ka, F1 0.863** — 0.7% of
ordinary segments sit above it and 4.0% of archaic ones below. That is the
ceiling any threshold-based detector is working against.

### Darwin mode

20 mutation proposals over 6 generations, **1 accepted** (the archaic depth
threshold, 620 → 551 ka). Held-out F1 **0.821 → 0.867**, precision 0.882 → 0.874,
recall **0.768 → 0.859**. The held-out gain (+0.046) exceeds the training gain
(+0.034), so the tuning found something about the problem rather than about the
training split.

### Flywheel

| Turn | Calls | Ghost | New refs | Precision | Recall | F1 |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 542 | 269 | 269 | 0.845 | 0.839 | 0.8419 |
| 2 | 542 | 269 | 0 | 0.845 | 0.839 | 0.8419 |
| 3 | 542 | 269 | 0 | 0.845 | 0.839 | 0.8419 |

Converged after 3 turns. **F1 did not move.** At this threshold setting the first
pass had already found everything the references could rescue, and the flywheel's
honest result is that it added nothing — which is the outcome a self-terminating
loop has to be able to report.

An earlier implementation *did* move the number, in the wrong direction: it
promoted any ghost call to a reference and rescued anything within
`ghost_link_ka` of one, so false positives validated more false positives and
precision fell 0.84 → 0.22 → 0.19 over three rounds. Two rules fixed it, both
from the population genetics rather than from tuning — only primary calls may be
promoted, and a rescue requires the segment to be closer to a known ghost than to
anyone living. `flywheel_does_not_bootstrap_on_its_own_errors` guards it.

### Retrieval

**406,530** exact divergence computations against **1,273,680** for the
equivalent all-pairs scan — **3.1×** less work for the same calls.

### The two lineages

| Mode | Median depth | Planted split | Holds | Oceanian share |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 0 · Ghost-A | 837 ka | 800 ka | 76.4% of all Ghost-A segments | 11.2% |
| 1 · Ghost-B | 2.03 Ma | 1.8 Ma | **100%** of all Ghost-B segments | **38.1%** |

Of 269 segments called GHOST, **183 (68%)** really came from one of the two ghost
lineages; the rest are Neanderthal and Denisovan segments whose depth ran deeper
than the reference-match margin.

**The modes do not separate cleanly, and the reason matters.** Ghost-A's own depth
distribution runs to 2.5 Ma, so its deep tail lands on top of Ghost-B. With only
18 super-archaic segments in the cohort, depth can tell you two sources are
present but cannot assign individual segments between them.

What does separate them is a signal that never touches the depth axis. Ghost-B
reached modern humans only through Denisovans, so it should concentrate where
Denisovan ancestry concentrates — and Oceania supplies **38.1%** of the deep
mode's segments against **11.2%** of the shallow one, a **3.4×** enrichment. That
is the fingerprint of an indirect route, and it is why the deep mode can be named
super-archaic even though its segments cannot be separated one by one.

## What this is not

- The genomes are **simulated** against a demography calibrated to published
  divergence times. This is not an analysis of 1000 Genomes, HGDP, or SGDP data,
  and it says nothing about any living person's ancestry.
- It reproduces the *reasoning* of an ARG-based method. It is not a
  reimplementation of TRACE, and the published results are theirs, not
  reproduced here.
- Each planted segment has exactly one carrier per window. That matches the low
  per-locus frequency of real archaic tracts and keeps ground truth unambiguous,
  but it sidesteps the harder real-world case where several people share the same
  introgressed haplotype.
- Research and educational software. Not a medical device.
